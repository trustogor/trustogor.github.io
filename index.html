My approach to talent is built on three core beliefs about hiring, leadership, and how AI is changing the game.

Belief 01

Hiring is a System, Not a Transaction

Every hire compounds. Good hiring builds momentum; bad hiring creates drag. You need processes that work repeatedly, not one-off wins.

Belief 02

Talent Leaders Must Operate at Two Altitudes

You set strategy and policy at the top, but you need to stay on the ground and own outcomes. The best insights come from both views.

Belief 03

AI Augments Hiring — It Doesn’t Replace It

AI is a tool for better sourcing, faster screening, and smarter ranking. But judgment, calibration, and cultural fit still require human insight.
