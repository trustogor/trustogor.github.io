The Problem
Nigeria's lending market is evolving rapidly, with significant demand from young professionals in their early and mid-level career stages. These professionals earn regularly but face cash flow constraints — managing multiple bills, emergencies, and personal expenses means utility payments often compete for limited available funds. Existing solutions are friction-heavy (long approval processes, high interest rates, collateral requirements) or unavailable entirely for utility-specific lending.
The Solution
uSwitch is built for this market. It's a fintech platform that makes utility lending frictionless for young professionals — instant credit access for electricity purchases, transparent repayment terms, and automated management so utility bills never trigger financial strain. It bridges the gap between regular income and irregular cash flow, giving users the flexibility to manage essential services without cascading financial pressure.
